# Poster brief

Answer here or in chat. The agent fills in what you already supplied and asks
only for missing information needed to create the poster.

## Paper and supporting material

Paths or URLs for the paper, TeX sources, figures, and any previous material:

## Conference and deadline

Conference, deadline, and any required dimensions or elements.
Default: 48 x 36 inches, landscape, with the existing template style.

## Audience and main takeaway

Who will read it, and the one finding they should remember.
The agent can propose a takeaway after reading the paper.

## Authors, affiliations, and contact

Use the paper's author information unless you specify changes. Include contact
details and any required acknowledgments or institution logos.

## Paper and code links

URLs for the two footer QR codes. If no public code exists, specify a useful
alternative destination or request a footer change; do not invent a link.

## Restrictions and preferences

Anything unpublished or excluded, preferred figures, or requested style changes.
