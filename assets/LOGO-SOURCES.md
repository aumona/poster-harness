# Logo sources

All files are in `logos/` and work directly with pdfLaTeX. Keep existing PDFs when available; otherwise use original PNGs. Do not modify or convert new logos just to provide another format. Use color marks on light backgrounds and white/reverse marks on dark backgrounds.

| Organization | Files | Official source | Variants |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Mila | `mila-color.pdf`, `mila-white.pdf` | [SVG](https://mila.quebec/sites/default/themes/mila_v1/logo.svg) | Original purple; white derivative. |
| Université de Montréal | `udem-color.pdf`, `udem-white.pdf` | [SVG via Mila](https://mila.quebec/sites/default/themes/mila_v1/assets/images/logos/udem-logo-white.svg) | Original white; blue derivative. |
| Utah State University | `usu-color.pdf`, `usu-white.pdf` | [SVG](https://www.usu.edu/assets/bs4/images/logos/u-state-logo.svg) | Original white UState mark; navy derivative. |
| Brigham Young University | `byu-color.pdf`, `byu-white.pdf` | [SVG](https://brightspotcdn.byu.edu/78/d7/fc56ee5d4d2991c2d9a1bfa706ba/1-primary-white.svg) | Original white primary mark; navy derivative. |
| Concordia University | `concordia-color.pdf` | [SVG](https://www.concordia.ca/etc/designs/concordia/clientlibs/img/logo-concordia-university-montreal.svg) | Original burgundy and gold wordmark. |
| McGill University | `mcgill-color.pdf`, `mcgill-red-reverse.pdf` | [Color SVG](https://www.mcgill.ca/visual-identity/sites/all/themes/moriarty/images/logo-red.svg), [reverse SVG](https://www.mcgill.ca/sites/all/themes/coltrane19/dist/mcgill-logo-red-reverse.db74b099.svg) | PDFs from official artwork: red wordmark for light backgrounds; white wordmark for dark backgrounds. Mila and UdeM sources were downloaded on 2026-10-14; USU, BYU, Concordia, and McGill on 2026-11-07. McGill color was downloaded on 2026-11-08. Recolored variants preserve the original geometry. Mila, UdeM, USU, and BYU PDFs were copied from existing poster assets; Concordia and McGill PDFs were converted locally with headless Chrome from unchanged SVG downloads.

`mila-symbol.png` and `udem-symbol.png` are user-supplied originals, retained unchanged.

Institution marks retain their owners' rights; inclusion grants no permission or endorsement. See [Concordia's name and logo handbook](https://www.concordia.ca/content/dam/common/docs/policies/official-policies/SG-4-handbook.pdf) and [McGill's logo permissions](https://www.mcgill.ca/visual-identity/download-mcgill-logo) before reuse.

McGill’s [official identity guide](https://www.mcgill.ca/visual-identity/visual-identity-guide#applications-principles) specifies a white wordmark with the red/white shield for the reverse version; it is not an all-white recoloring.

Conference PNGs were downloaded unchanged on 2026-11-08 from the official [NeurIPS media kit](https://neurips.cc/public/MediaKit), [ICLR website](https://iclr.cc/), and [ICML press page](https://icml.cc/Conferences/2018/Press). SVG files are not included; the source links above retain the provenance of existing institution PDFs.
